# Bluport Brand Asset Folders

This directory holds all real photography and visual assets for the Bluport website.
The current production build includes temporary atmospheric stock visuals for layout polish.
Replace these with original Bluport-owned photos as soon as the final media library is ready.

Place the primary white Bluport logo at public/images/brand/bpl-logo-white.svg.
Optional fallback logo path: public/images/brand/bpl-logo-white.png.

Current temporary stock files in use:

- fleet/heavy-equipment-transport.jpg

## /brand/
Logo lockups, brand mark variations, wordmark exports (PNG/SVG, dark and light versions).
- bluport-logo-dark.svg
- bluport-logo-light.svg
- bpl-mark.svg

## /fleet/
Photos of the Bluport truck and trailer.
- F350 profile shots
- 40' power tilt trailer deck and mechanism
- Securement details: straps, chains, binders, load locks
- Pre-trip inspection photos

## /loads/
Photos of equipment being transported or staged.
- Loaded machines: skid steers, excavators, boom lifts, telehandlers, generators, light towers
- Before/after loaded and unloaded sequences
- Pickup at EquipmentShare yards or customer locations
- Delivery at active jobsites

## /jobsites/
Photos taken at customer sites and work environments.
- Construction site entrances
- Utility and infrastructure work
- Data center construction environments
- Industrial yards
- Equipment staged near active crews

## /team/
Photos of the Bluport team and operations.
- Driver portrait (professional, outdoor or truck context)
- Dispatch and operations workspace
- Veteran-led team image (no staged stock vibes)
- Safety and securement checklist

## /backgrounds/
Full-bleed hero imagery for section backgrounds.
- Cinematic truck/trailer at sunrise or blue hour (wide landscape, 16:9 or wider)
- Low-angle front quarter F350 shot
- Motion blur / road shots for hero loop fallback frame

## ../video/
Short video clips for hero loop and content sections.
- hero-loop.mp4 — 8–12 second seamless loop, truck/trailer in motion
- loading-sequence.mp4 — Tilt trailer loading/unloading operation
- securement-detail.mp4 — Close-up of straps and chains being set
- delivery-approach.mp4 — Drone or gimbal shot arriving at jobsite
